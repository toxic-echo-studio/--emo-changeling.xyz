Hi! It's the Zebroid

Being able to somewhat draw gives too much power to wrong people.

I should also probably come back to reality more often.

Ehhh. REALITY BORING

One day I woke up with a concept in my head: ‘damn, I met that band in some other universe and threatened to turn them into birds and post it on the Earth’s internet’

I wonder what annoyed me this much xP

Well,that led to creation of an …image.. I want to erase from my memory. (Its not this one!)

I literally made this as a penance and apology of sorts lol

Some of my thought processes:

I imagined Corky as an elegant fantail pigeon (MY ARTISTIC FREEDOM!!!!!!) and honestly I choose that specific bass to trace (yes, I committed THE crime because I couldn’t draw the instruments correctly) just because I liked the star. I never drew birds like that before and I think the next one is a lot better) SHINY FEATHERS

—-----------------------------------------------------------------------------—---------

Evan to me absolutely gives a vibe of a sassy capuchine pigeon

(but still HAD to be shiny.)

  • ‘How would a pigeon even wear a vest?’

Asked Cam to me in a vision. Good question. It led to a whole debate that ended up with Evan scribbling over visualisation of my sketch and making it look far better than first version (no, I WILL NOT show you what it looked before, I deleted that forever)

(what the hell…)

No tattoos because… honestly I didn’t even try. I couldn’t even portray them on a larger wing, this is beyond my abilities (this is why none of you have tattoos here) especially at the time. I didn’t draw much and the pigeon series kind of reactivated me, I’d say im in the process of learning ok
