   What else is commonly associated with vampires? Blood of course.
And so it happened, that while I was having a hard time deciding which sketch I want to use for the final piece, the ‘confession’ dropped (its so GOATed as pride month song btw, you should’ve waited until june >:C ) and it was kinda a ‘voila!’ moment.

So very obviously I referenced the song because while most modern depictions of vampires paint them as those mysterious, dignified and eerily calm figures, they are still dangerous and in some cultures - violent.

I essentially envisioned that the chalice is full of blood of those who got under your skin. I also wanted this piece to refer to ‘Confession’ because I maybe if it gets commemorated properly we will get more of your singing in future releases :P
