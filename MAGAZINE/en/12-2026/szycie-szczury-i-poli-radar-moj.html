Sewing, Rats and Poly-Radars: My Squirrel Brain

Today is pure chaos. I'm trying to fix an Amazon skirt (yeah, I buy stuff there sometimes, I'm a poser, go easy on me), while showing off a necklace made of safety pins and literal trash from the studio. Someone gonna tell me DIY is hard and they are too broke for it? BS. While sewing, my ADHD brain goes full squirrel mode - I lose my thread, fight for survival with a needle, and in the background, some idiot left a hair straightener plugged into the basement outlet for two weeks. Major DID moment.

I'm also going on a rant about stuff that's been triggering me online lately. First: treating animals like objects. Why do people have such a hard time with basic respect for other species? Rats have social structures almost identical to humans, and calling yourself a dog's "owner" is just cringe. An animal is your roommate, not a slave.

Second: polyamory. Seriously, are people pretending to be dumber than they actually are, or is it some inside joke? Questions like "why do all you poly people look the same" are pure comedy, and my poly-radar keeps glitching anyway, so it would save me a lot of heartbreaks if that were actually true. Throw in getting brainwashed by Duolingo, which traps you in a dopamine loop and leaves you unable to actually speak the language... and you get the full picture of my mental state today. Did I fix the skirt? Spoiler: I failed. But at least my necklace is awesome. Stay scene, stay weird!
