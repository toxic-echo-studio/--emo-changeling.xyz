Moshing with Priests, Concert Trash & Niche Bands

Let's start with the fact that I once had the chance to mosh with a bunch of priests in combat boots at some religious event. But onto the important stuff. Bring Me The Horizon is an absolute staple for me (and yes, we are all pretending the "Amo" album was just a glitch in reality and never happened). Unfortunately, modern concert culture is turning into total trash. A while ago, Oli Sykes got hit in the temple with a phone on stage. Seriously, how miserable do you have to be to pull something like that? Because of this kind of behavior, venues drop ridiculous rules on us, like banning corpse paint at metal shows.

Another cringe trend is bragging about skipping opening acts. People proudly post about only showing up for the headliner, skipping awesome opening bands. You are wasting your money and just flexing your musical ignorance. For example, I had mediocre expectations for Taylor Acorn after hearing her studio albums, but live, they absolutely killed it, and the guitarist was going so crazy he almost knocked her off the stage.

Thanks to today's music industry and streaming, our brains are so fried that if a track doesn't hook us in 15 seconds, it goes in the trash. You have to learn how to listen to music. I had the same experience with Noahfinnce - at first, his synthetic vocals didn't click with me, and now he is one of my favorite artists. Do yourselves a favor and give niche creators a chance. And if anyone says I am doing this shoutout just to clout-chase, I will personally come over and permanently glue f*cking spiders to your ceilings. That is a threat.
