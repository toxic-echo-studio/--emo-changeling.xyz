LARPing Paid Collabs, Alcohol-Soaked Hardware & Focus Battles: The Unboxing Interlude

After some heavy, deeply personal logs, it's time for a quick breather and a vibe reset. Before the second part of our psych ward history drops, we're throwing our very first, completely unhinged piercing jewelry unboxing onto the table. Real talk: the internet has been obsessed with misusing the word "LARP" lately, slapping it onto stuff where it literally means the polar opposite. So today, we're doing a literal Live Action Role Play - pretending we're famous and cool enough for brands to send us free PR, when in reality, the "paid collab" was just us paying them for the hardware.

This unboxing had every possible fail: fighting the camera's focus depth, hunting for a non-existent visual sweet spot, and aggressively opening the package with a razor blade purely for cheap shock value (even though the box could've been ripped open by hand). On the menu: a rainbow titanium setup for the upcoming pride parade, spare septum horseshoes meant for archery safety (since snagging a released bowstring on a 1.6mm nose ring sounds like a horrific involuntary body mod), and the mandatory sticker loot. Highlights include a glittery rat sticker reading "Bite Me," which directly feeds our imaginary band The Half Dozen Rats in the Bucket.

Let's clear something up though: actual LARPing in subcultures isn't about being a clueless poser. A dedicated LARPer playing an emo kid would literally memorize full discographies of early emocore bands just to be 100% prepared for any callouts. As for this unboxing? Zero corporate polish, just raw honesty, past DIY piercing mistakes, and pure ADHD chaos from people who completely lack math and geometry skills.

Stay scene, stay weird... and fr, leave the piercing needles to the pros! 🖤🐀
