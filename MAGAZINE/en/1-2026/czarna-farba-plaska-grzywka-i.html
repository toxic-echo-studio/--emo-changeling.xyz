Black Paint, Flat Bangs & The Late-Capitalism Dystopia: What Being "Alt" Actually Means

You turn on the basement RGBs, grab a dry brush, take a random wooden box, and try to make sense of a world that's honestly ready for the trash heap. Standard procedure, right? Except the moment black paint starts dripping down your hands (and literal face), a basic DIY setup turns into a low-key existential breakdown about how cooked we all are.

Let's talk about fast fashion monstrosities like Shein and Zara. Everyone knows these giant corps are out here speedrunning new ways to blend cheap nylon and plastic just so your fit disintegrates in two weeks. But does slapping an "alt" label on yourself automatically clear your hands? Yeah, not really.

Here's the hot take nobody wants to hear: secondhand shopping on Vinted can literally just be a placebo to make your moral ego feel cute. Scoring a huge thrift haul to "save the planet"? Cool flex, but are you realizing you're just freeing up closet space for someone who's straight-up addicted to fueling Shein overconsumption?

We live under late-stage capitalism - slave labor and eco-destruction are baked into basically everything we touch. You can't fully escape the grid unless a giant asteroid hits and resets earth. But there's still a massive difference between buying one solid piece every 3 years vs. dropping half your paycheck at fast-fashion racks every single month.

Bottom line: stop gatekeeping being "alt" like it makes you a saint. Being alternative just means thinking and existing outside the brainwashed mainstream - and newsflash, alt people can be total trash human beings too.

Stay scene, stay weird... and fr, don't eat the paint. 🖤🦖
