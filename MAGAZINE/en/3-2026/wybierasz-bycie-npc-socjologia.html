Do You Choose to Be an NPC? Sociology, Pop-Psychology Trash & Fronting Chaos

"You can't be alt if you make fun of normies" - fr, who even falls for this gatekeeping poser logic? Let's set the record straight: nobody's out here hating on regular people just to look edgy. It's a direct rant against the system that takes potentially bright, expressive humans and compresses them into dull, predictable clones. Most normies are literally just victims of social engineering, peer pressure, and the existential dread of "what will people think." Everyone gets flashes of wanting to break free, but most people suppress them because staying in line feels safer.

If I could force one class into the high school curriculum, it'd be practical sociology with social engineering workshops. Society would be infinitely less cooked if people actually understood how they're being manipulated on a daily basis. But do yourself a favor: stay away from pop psychology. Self-help books and TikTok coaching brainrot will literally just make you dumber. Hit the actual academic databases and research papers if you actually want to deprogram yourself.

Meanwhile, behind the scenes? Absolute system chaos. As the final layer of black paint goes onto the infamous box (now as black as our literal future), the inner Twitch Chat bleeds directly into the front. The result? Hot takes on throwing the entire concept of gender into the trash, callouts on fake feminism, random internal roasting, and sweet, sweet real-time dissociation. Project Box is officially wrapped, but the bottom line stays: you only get one run with this exact set of memories. Stop acting like an NPC.

Stay scene, stay weird... and fr, stop consuming pop-psychology trash! 🖤📦
