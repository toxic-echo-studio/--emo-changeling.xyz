TX2 Historical Tour Archive (2018-2026)

← Back to Current & Upcoming Tour Schedule (2026-2027)

This archive documents TX2 live dates prior to the summer 2026 European festival run (starting 2026-06-05), compiled from concert databases, promoter announcements, ticketing archives, and press coverage.

Missing dates or corrections? If you have reliable information, ticket stubs, flyers, or sources that could supplement this concert archive or correct an error, please write to tx2pigeon@emo-changeling.xyz.

Color Code Legend & Verification Thresholds

  • Verified 2+ independent reliable sources (official tour graphics, promoter/venue listings, press coverage, or corroborated setlist/archive records).
  • High Confidence 1 reliable source or 3+ weak identical sources matching date, city, and venue without conflicting data.
  • Unverified or Disputed Circumstantial evidence, fewer than 3 weak identical sources, or unresolved date/venue conflict.
