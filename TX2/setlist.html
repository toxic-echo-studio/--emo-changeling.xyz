TX2 Live Setlists: 2026 Tour & Festival Archive

This page documents the verified live setlists performed by TX2 from the 2026 European Summer Festival Run through Louder Than Life 2026 and the autumn 2026 Not Broken Enough Tour. All entries reflect public concert logs, fan-documented setlists, and press coverage.

1. Not Broken Enough Tour (Oct-Dec 2026)

Performing as direct support for Catch Your Breath across North America, TX2 operated within a tight 35-minute support slot. The 11-track setlist introduced the new single "Witch Hunt" into the live rotation, while older staples such as "Randy McNally (No Love Like Christian Hate)" and "Degrade Me" were rotated out.

  6. Hollow Frame
  7. Witch Hunt NEW!

  11. I Would Hate Me Too
