TX2 Live Setlists: 2018-2026 Tour & Festival Archive

This page documents TX2 live setlists from early DIY shows (2018-2022) and 2023-2026 club, support, and festival runs through the 2026 European Summer Festival Run, Louder Than Life 2026, and the autumn 2026 Not Broken Enough Tour. All entries reflect public concert logs, fan-documented setlists, and press coverage.

Have reliable setlist notes, photos of physical setlists, concert flyers, or corrections for missing or partial entries? Contact us at tx2pigeon@emo-changeling.xyz so we can expand or fix this archive.

1. Not Broken Enough Tour (Oct-Dec 2026)

Performing as direct support for Catch Your Breath across North America, TX2 operated within a tight 35-minute support slot. The 11-track setlist introduced the new single "Witch Hunt" into the live rotation, while older staples such as "Randy McNally (No Love Like Christian Hate)" and "Degrade Me" were rotated out.

  1. Infamous
  2. Black Wedding
  3. Vendetta
  4. HOSTAGE (they will not erase us)
  5. Feed
  6. Hollow Frame
  7. Witch Hunt NEW!
  8. So Numb
  9. Confession
  10. MAD
  11. I Would Hate Me Too

2. Louder Than Life 2026 (Sep 19, 2026)

TX2 appeared at Louder Than Life in Louisville, Kentucky on September 19, 2026. Serving as a pre-"Witch Hunt" US festival bridge slot between the European summer run and the autumn arena/club support dates, the set focused on the band's core 10-track live rotation.

5. Black Veil Brides - North American Tour + US Spring Festivals (Apr 25 - May 30, 2026)

Strong sources (13 individual show logs on setlist.fm). Treat this run as one tour with a clear mid-tour setlist shift: during the early leg (Apr 26 - May 6: Reno, Seattle, La Vista, Houston, San Antonio), TX2 rotated Pickup Truck in the main set (and opened Reno and Seattle with a cover of Limp Bizkit's "Break Stuff"; Houston omitted "So Numb" for a 7-song slot, while Seattle added "Black Wedding" as track 3). From May 11 onward (Greensboro, Philadelphia, Madison, Toronto, Montreal, New York, Silver Spring, Worcester), "Confession" replaced "Pickup Truck" in a standardized 8-song support set. Two late-leg single-show anomalies occurred: New York (May 28, Palladium Times Square) opened with "Break Stuff" (9 songs), and Pittsburgh (May 15, Stage AE) expanded to an 11-song set adding "Murder Scene", "Infamous", and both "Confession" and "Pickup Truck". No separate song-by-song setlists are archived for Welcome to Rockville (May 9), Sonic Temple (May 16), or unlogged stops (Riverside, Salt Lake City, Denver, Dallas, Atlanta, Norfolk, Detroit, Indianapolis, Minneapolis, Chicago).

  1. Feed
  2. Vendetta
  3. HOSTAGE (they will not erase us)
  4. So Numb
  5. The End of Us
  6. Confession
  7. MAD
  8. I Would Hate Me Too

20. 2023 Support Runs & Club Shows (Apr 19 - Nov 24, 2023)

Weak / fragmentary sources or no setlist found: no complete, verified song-by-song setlists exist in public archives for TX2's 2023 dates. However, individual concert logs and press reviews document specific songs and live debuts across these 2023 runs:
