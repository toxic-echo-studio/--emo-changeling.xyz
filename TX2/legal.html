📜 FAN PROJECT RULES: "TX2 Pigeonification Ritual"

§1. What This Project Is (and What It Isn't)

This is a 100% fan-made, non-profit, community project.

The organizers are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or linked to the band TX2, its members, label, management, or tour promoters.

This is not an official fan club and nobody is making any money off this.

§2. Contact & Official Channel

The only official contact point for the project is: tx2pigeon@emo-changeling.xyz.

This address is used strictly and exclusively for artwork submissions and formal project-related communication. Any personal inquiries, casual chat, or spam sent to this address will be ignored.

§3. Funding & Costs

Joining in is completely free.

We don't charge any fees, and we don't pay for submitted artwork.

All costs (collecting artwork, layout, printing the physical art pieces and poster) are covered out of our own pockets.

We do not accept donations or financial contributions.

Submitted art, physical prints, and the poster will never be sold, put behind a paywall, or commercialized.

§4. Content Guidelines & Right of Rejection

We reserve the absolute right to reject any submission that we deem inappropriate, offensive, sexually explicit or provocative, unlawful, hate speech, or promoting illegal acts and acts of violence. Keep it respectful.

§5. Copyright, Image Rights & Band Outreach

You keep full rights to your work. By submitting, you confirm that you are the sole creator of the artwork and aren't stealing anyone else's IP.

By sending us your work, you give us a free, non-exclusive, non-commercial pass to:

  • Edit the file digitally and physically only as needed for printing (layout, color correction, cropping).
  • Print your work one time as part of:
    • 1 individual physical print of your artwork.
    • 1 copy of the group poster.
  • Hand over the physical prints and poster to the members of TX2 during the VIP meet & greet.

Credit: You agree to let us put your name, handle, or signature next to your art on the physical prints and on the poster.

No Online Uploads: We will not post your submitted artwork online or on the project website.

Digital Access & Contact Info for the Band: Digital access means only viewing/downloading the files you submitted. You agree to let us share digital access with TX2 and their crew for original files and print layouts.

By default, your submission email address will also be shared with TX2 alongside your artwork. This is solely to allow the band or their team to contact you directly regarding copyrights, licensing, or official interest in your art.

Opt-Out Option: If you do not want your email shared with the band, you can opt out by explicitly stating so in your submission email, or by emailing us from that same address before January 1, 2027.

Strictly Non-Commercial: This permission does not allow us or any third party to sell, license, or profit off your work.

What the Band Can Do (Fair Use / Non-Commercial): You allow the members of TX2 to show off your work for free (e.g., displaying the physical art in vlogs, social media stories, behind-the-scenes videos, or keeping it in their personal collection). They cannot use your art as official merch, album art for a paid release, or in paid ad campaigns without reaching out to you directly for a separate agreement.

§6. Privacy & Personal Data

We respect your privacy. Your personal data (including email addresses and names) will never be processed or used for any purpose other than organizing and running this specific project.

All participant personal data stored on our website resources will be permanently deleted immediately after the submission phase wraps up on February 21, 2027.
