🔮 THE PIGEONIFICATION RITUAL: HOW TO PARTICIPATE

To ascend TX2 to their highest bird form, we need as many creative mages as possible. Are you in?

📜 What we need for your spell:

  • Your Fanart: A drawing, painting, digital art, collage, or paper cutout depicting at least one member of TX2 as a pigeon.
  • A Short Message: 2-3 sentences straight from the heart (included in the text of your email).
  • Your Signature: Your nickname, tag, or social media handle so they know who cast it.

🛠️ Technical Requirements & Guidelines:

  • All Techniques Welcome: Digital art, traditional pencil/ink, painting, papercraft-whatever you love.
  • Photo / Scan Quality: If you’re submitting a photo of traditional art, please snap it in good, bright light (avoid dark shadows!). Aim for a minimum resolution of 5 Megapixels (around 2000×2000 px) so it prints crisp.
  • File Size: Keep your email attachments under 200 MB.
  • Minor Edits: We reserve the right to tweak backgrounds, framing, or slightly brighten dark photos just to make sure every artwork pops and fits cohesively onto the final prints and poster.

📩 How & When to Send:

Send your spells to: tx2pigeon@emo-changeling.xyz

Deadline: November 30th, 2026 (with possible extension if needed).

When the time comes, we will stitch all your spells together into physical prints and a massive, gorgeous group poster to hand-deliver to TX2 during the VIP meeting in Warsaw!

Let the power of creativity and our shared pride seal their fate as majestic birds. 🕊️
