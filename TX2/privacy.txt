TX2 Unofficial Fansite - Privacy Notice
Version: 1.2.3
Last Updated: 2026-11-22

Project: TX2 Unofficial Fansite ("TX2 Pigeonification Ritual")
Co-creators: Nico Łach (Zebroid) and Mighn Taghor
Voluntary Hosting & Technical Infrastructure: Toxic Echo Studio (non-commercial)

1. No Accounts, Cookies, or Client-Side Tracking
This static fansite has no sign-up forms, user accounts, or newsletters.
It sets zero cookies and uses zero sessionStorage or tracking profiles.
Language versions are served as separate static HTML documents in English (/) and Polish (/pl/).

2. Fan Project Artwork Submissions & GDPR Disclosures
Artwork submissions sent by email to tx2pigeon@emo-changeling.xyz are processed solely for organizing the fan project and producing the physical prints and group poster.
By default, submission email addresses and submitted artwork are shared with TX2 and their crew solely for copyright, attribution, or direct licensing/official interest inquiries.
Participants may opt out of sharing their email address with the band before January 1, 2027, as detailed in the project rules at:
- https://tx2.emo-changeling.xyz/legal
- https://tx2.emo-changeling.xyz/pl/legal
All participant personal data stored on website resources will be permanently deleted after the submission phase concludes on February 21, 2027.

3. Hosting Logs & Temporary Technical Monitoring
The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which processes standard technical server access logs required for delivery and security.
Following major codebase or structural updates, temporary cookieless Cloudflare Real User Measurement (RUM) may be enabled under legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) strictly to gather aggregated performance metrics and debug technical issues without storing or reading cookies or local storage data.

4. Fonts, UI & Client-Side Scripts
The interface is built with pure HTML and CSS plus two lightweight optional scripts:
- lightbox.js (fanart modal preview on the homepage)
- tours.js (tour status indicators on /tours and /pl/tours)
Typography is served locally using self-hosted Inter WOFF2 font files (inter-latin.woff2 and inter-latin-ext.woff2), with zero external requests to Google Fonts or third-party font servers.

5. Embedded Third-Party Media
The /media and /pl/media pages embed external media players from YouTube (via privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com iframes with lazy loading) and TikTok.

6. Contact
For privacy, opt-out, or content inquiries, contact: tx2pigeon@emo-changeling.xyz, admin@toxic-echo.studio
