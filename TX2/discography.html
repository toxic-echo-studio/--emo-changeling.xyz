TX2 Complete Discography (2015-2026)

From early DIY emo-rap mixtapes recorded in Colorado to Hopeless Records studio albums, viral singles, guest appearances, and acoustic livestream demos. Below is the complete catalog of TX2 releases organized chronologically, including tracklists, individual track release dates, song lengths, and physical editions marked with 💿.

Missing dates, tracklists, or corrections? Have reliable information about TX2 releases, track lengths, physical editions, or corrections for missing or partial entries? Contact us at tx2pigeon@emo-changeling.xyz so we can expand or fix this archive.

Archive Legend:

  • 💿 CD / Vinyl - Confirmed physical edition (Vinyl, CD, or limited physical pressing).
  • Tracklist Dates - Individual track premiere date when a song was released as a single prior to the full album or EP.
  • Pre-2022 Archival Notes - Early DIY projects removed from major streaming services before the pop-punk rebrand; incomplete tracklists or runtimes are marked accordingly.

Pełna dyskografia TX2 (2015-2026)
