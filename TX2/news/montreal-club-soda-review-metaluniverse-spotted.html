Found Online: Even Montreal Skeptics Couldn’t Look Away (And Yes, Part of the Crowd Bounced Right After TX2)

Author: Mighn Taghor | Published: 2026-11-22

Whenever Reddit puts that tired old record back on - claiming TX2 is “just algorithms and empty TikTok hype” - it pays to look at write-ups from actual clubs, especially the ones coming from outside the fan bubble. A killer take just popped up on the Canadian outlet MetalUniverse.net, where Molly Caisse-Vee reviewed the show at Montreal’s Club Soda (Nov 17).

The best part? She admits right out of the gate that TX2’s music isn’t really her cup of tea - which makes what she saw from behind the lens hit even harder. Even though Evan and the crew were “only” opening for Catch Your Breath, something went down on the floor that sums up just how big this whole thing has gotten:

  • A show inside a show: TX2 didn’t have to beg for the headliner’s crowd to pay attention. They brought out their own fiercely loyal crew who screamed back every single lyric on the setlist, caught the band for a quick hangout near the entrance during the intermission, and then… literally headed straight home as soon as TX2’s set wrapped. A pretty loud reality check for anyone who thinks an opener is just there as background noise.
  • A straight time-warp to the VIVA / MTV era: Molly also called out the band’s visual bite - all black and red, rocking that slightly provocative skater-kid vibe straight out of 2000s music videos (she compared it to classics on Canada’s MusiquePlus, which for us back home screams late-night VIVA and old-school MTV).

Our take: Love or hate Evan Thomas’s whole persona, impartial reviews like this expose why the 2027 European tour is chewing through ticket tiers so fast. When a metal-site reviewer walks in thinking “not really my sound,” still gives props for stage presence, and has to note that plenty of fans showed up exclusively for the support act - it means this machine actually delivers live, not just in vertical reels on your phone screen.
