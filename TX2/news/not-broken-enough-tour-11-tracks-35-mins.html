Not Broken Enough Tour Midway: 11 Tracks Crushed Into 35 Minutes

Author: Mighn Taghor | Published: 2026-11-08

Hitting Atlanta's Tabernacle on November 8 alongside Catch Your Breath, TX2 has solved the live setlist equation. Packing 11 songs (starting with 'Infamous' and closing on 'I Would Hate Me Too') into an unforgiving 35-minute support window requires brutal discipline: zero dead air between tracks, seamless beat-matched transitions, and trimmed second verses to prioritize massive choruses and crushing breakdowns. If this nonstop adrenaline carries over to the European run, Warsaw is in for absolute mayhem - check out the fan campaign on our main page.
