New Collab Drop: TX2 Guests on Bailey Spinn's 'silent anymore'

Author: Mighn Taghor | Published: 2026-11-13

On November 13, rising alt-rock vocalist Bailey Spinn dropped her heaviest sophomore record to date, 'death & devotion' (via AWAL), featuring TX2 on track #8, 'silent anymore'. Sharing guest billing with Kellin Quinn (Sleeping With Sirens), The Funeral Portrait, and Melrose Avenue, Evan Thomas and crew bring gritty crossover weight to the track while already fueling social feeds with unhinged nurse-costume Shorts alongside Bailey on YouTube and X. Marking another sharp link-up with female voices in modern heavy alt-rock following DeathbyRomy on 'Feed', this drop keeps momentum rolling before the upcoming tour dates and joins the catalog tracked in our discography.
