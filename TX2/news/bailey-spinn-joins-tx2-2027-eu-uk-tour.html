Bailey Spinn Joins TX2 for Their 2027 European & UK Tour

Author: Mighn Taghor | Published: 2026-11-21

We finally know who's warming up the crowd for TX2's upcoming invasion of Europe. Taking the stage right before Evan Thomas and the crew is American singer-songwriter Bailey Spinn. This run will mark her very first time playing live across Europe and the UK.

Who Is Bailey Spinn and Where'd She Come From?

Born in 2002 and hailing from Virginia, Bailey Rose Spinnenweber took the classic modern route: straight from quick internet virals to live stages. Back in 2020, she blew up on TikTok with short POV skits, pulling in millions of followers within a few months before dropping out of her marketing degree to go all-in on social media. Music came later - first she built up momentum with YouTube covers (her take on Olivia Rodrigo's "Traitor" racked up 1.5 million views in a single month), and by 2023 she kicked off her own originals, starting with the single "romance is dead" and the "my worst enemy" EP.

Even though the music press loves slapping pop-punk and alt-rock labels on her - throwing around names like Evanescence, Paramore, and Sleeping With Sirens as influences - her actual tracks feel super polished and pretty one-note. Following her 2025 debut album "loser", she dropped her sophomore record, "death & devotion", on November 13, 2026. Despite some solid guest spots from familiar faces in the scene - including Kellin Quinn (Sleeping With Sirens), Lee Jennings (The Funeral Portrait), Vlado Saric (Melrose Avenue), and TX2 himself on the four-minute track "silent anymore" - the whole thing stays locked in a very safe, soft-radio lane. It's the kind of sound that fits a morning traffic jam in the American suburbs way better than the raw, untamed rebellion Evan brings to the stage night after night.

The Collab and the Warsaw Stop

Their studio fling on "silent anymore" worked out well enough to spark a joint tour. When the mid-November announcements rolled out, Evan Thomas put it bluntly: after years of watching each other come up in the scene, they finally get to take it over together - kicking off with Bailey's first-ever run across Europe and the UK. She's locked in for both the March UK leg and mainland dates, including the sole Polish stop tracked in our upcoming tour dates:

  • When: February 20, 2027 (Saturday)
  • Where: Warsaw, Hydrozagadka Club (11 Listopada 22)
  • Lineup: TX2 (headliner) + Bailey Spinn (support)

For the local crew quietly hoping for a homegrown opener to kick things off at Hydrozagadka, this pick might come as a bit of a curveball. Looking at the pacing of the night, though, the setup is pretty straightforward: the American singer's tidy, predictable set will serve as a sharp contrast to the genre-bending chaos TX2 unleashes live. In practice, we're getting a chill, easygoing appetizer that'll let everyone save their energy for the absolute riot the headliner is guaranteed to bring.
